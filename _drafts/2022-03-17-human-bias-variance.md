The bias-variance decomposition is a foundational method for analyzing the
expected generalization error of a machine learning system as a sum of three
terms: the bias, the variance and an irreducible error. The usefulness of this
decomposition lies in the clear prescriptions available for reducing the
magnitude of each of the terms. What if the performance of humans on labeling
tasks could be analyzed in an equivalent fashion? What would be the
corresponding actions for reducing each term in the human error?
